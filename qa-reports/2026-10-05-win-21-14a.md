```text

local header の MS-DOS 欄（バイト 10-13 を読んだ）: 2019-03-01 00:00:00
```

`Start-Process -Wait filer.exe "in7" --keys "e<State:after><Quit>"` → `keys: done`、`toast: Unpacked into x\`、`hovered: …\in7\x`。

```text
in7\x\f.txt  2019-02-28T23:59:59.0000000+09:00
```

DOS 欄は `2019-03-01 00:00:00` なので、`23:59:59` は 7-Zip の NTFS 欄から来ている。行の期待どおり。

`pk\to-pack\b.txt` を `2021-06-15 12:34:57`、`pk\to-pack\f.txt` を `2019-02-28 23:59:59` にし、
`Start-Process -Wait filer.exe "pk" --keys "E<Enter><State:packed><Quit>"` → `keys: done`、`toast: Packed into to-pack.zip`。

```text
7z l -slt to-pack.zip:
  to-pack\b.txt  Modified = 2021-06-15 12:34:57  Characteristics = UT:M:1
  to-pack\f.txt  Modified = 2019-02-28 23:59:59  Characteristics = UT:M:1
中央ディレクトリの MS-DOS 欄:
  to-pack/b.txt  2021-06-15 12:34:58
  to-pack/f.txt  2019-03-01 00:00:00
7z x to-pack.zip -oout:
  out\to-pack\b.txt  2021-06-15T12:34:57.0000000+09:00
```
