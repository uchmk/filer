## Proposals

1. **Answering "no" to an overwrite should not silently throw the cut away.** What happened: `x` on
   `a-src\dup.txt`, `p` in `b-dst`, `s` (Skip). Nothing moved, the box closed, and **no toast was
   raised at all** — `toasts:` after the answer is still just `Yanked 1 item(s) (cut)`. The only way
   to learn the cut is gone is to press `p` again and be told `Nothing to paste`. Pressing Cancel is
   the same. So the user's reasonable next move — "fine, I'll put it somewhere else" — walks into a
   dead key, and the file they meant to move is still sitting where it was with nothing marking it.
   What should change: `paste()` (app.rs:3340) clears the register at submit; clear it instead when
   `OpEvent::Finished` reports a non-empty `moved`, and leave it armed when the job moved nothing.
   Failing that, say it: a toast like `Nothing moved — the cut was spent` when a `Move` job finishes
   with `moved` empty. Why: a cut you cannot see and cannot paste is the worst of both; and this is
   the only register operation in the program that can be lost without a word. How big: a few lines
   in the `Finished` arm plus the one line in `paste()` — but which of the two behaviours is right is
   a design call, so probably a QUESTIONS.md item.
2. **The state report should carry the yank register.** `state_report` has `selected:`, `hovered:`,
   `confirm:`, `toasts:` — and nothing for the register, which is the whole subject of section 10.
   Settling 10.9 therefore needed a `<Shot:>` of the header and a crop-and-read, for a number the
   program already holds exactly (`app.yank.paths.len()` and `app.yank.cut`). A line
   `yank: 1 cut` / `yank: 2 copied` / `yank: none` would make 10.1–10.7 readable text as well, and
   the picture would be needed only for the three colours — which is exactly what the section says is
   left for an eye. Why: the same shape as `pick:` and `confirm:` (#230), and those are what stopped
   those rows being pictures. How big: two lines in `state_report`.
3. **`1 items` and `item(s)`.** Read straight off this run's header (`1 cut · 1 items`) and its first
   toast (`Yanked 1 item(s) (cut)`). `summary()` (ui/mod.rs:82) always writes `{total} items`, and the
   yank toast writes `item(s)` rather than choosing. Both sit in the top-left of every screenshot this
   lane takes. What should change: `if total == 1 { "1 item" }`, and the toast to pick between `item`
   and `items`. Why: it is the most-read string in the program and it is ungrammatical in the single
   commonest case, pressing a key on one file. How big: one line each; the frame tests that assert on
   `19 items` and `1 copied · 19 items` are unaffected, but any asserting on a one-item header would
   need the new wording.
4. **The overwrite box should say what will become of the register.** Its body is `src:` and `dest:`
   — two paths — and the six buttons. Nothing in it hints that Skip and Cancel both end with an empty
   cut register, which is the one consequence a user cannot undo by pressing a key. A third body line
   on a move — `the cut will be cleared either way` — would cost a line and remove the surprise that
   proposal 1 is about. How big: a line in the `OpEvent::Conflict` arm (app.rs:3651), conditional on
   `OpKind::Move`; `confirm:` in the state report picks it up for free.
