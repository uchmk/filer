### Proposals

1. **`e` on a folder calls it a file.** In the first loop of this run the cursor
   was on the folder `inner` (my mistake), and `e` said `The file under the cursor
   is not an archive filer can read (zip, tar, tar.gz, tgz, 7z)`. It should say
   what it found: `inner\ is a folder -- e unpacks an archive (zip, tar, tar.gz,
   tgz, 7z)`, or, closer to what someone means by `e` on a folder, hint at `E`
   (`… to pack it, press E`). Why: "file" sent me to check whether the cursor was
   on the archive at all. Size: a line where the message is built.
2. **The state file's `preview top: 0 of 14` does not say how long the preview
   is.** For the 30-line `a.txt` it read `0 of 14`, the last top, not the line
   count; to tell whether 14 is right you need the visible height, which no line
   gives. A `preview lines: 30` line (or `0 of 14, 30 lines`) would let rows such
   as 21.21 and 41.x compare the preview against `(Get-Content f).Count`. Size: a
   line in the state writer.
