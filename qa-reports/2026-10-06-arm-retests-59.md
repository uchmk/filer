## Findings

1. **`<Drag:x0,y0-x1,y1>` selects nothing in the terminal pane.** `pointer_events` (`src/keyscript.rs:200`) puts the
   press, two moves and the release into one frame's `raw_input.events` (`src/main.rs:919`), so the pane never sees a
   frame with the button held -- the same drag with `SendInput`, five moves 60 ms apart, copies `ABCDE`. The x64 queue
   sends the next run to 1.11-1.11c with `<Drag:>`; it will read nothing there either, and could take that for a
   failure of the pane. Proposal 1.
2. (x64's #282 finding 2, seen here too) the opener toast still joins `cmd`'s two lines with a space after `、`.
